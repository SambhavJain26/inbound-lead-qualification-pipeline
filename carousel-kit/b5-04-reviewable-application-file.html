Review-file checklist · Synthetic demo basis01
The household match

Start with a
stable identity

Match household and property through trusted references before processing documents.

Ambiguous matches need human review; a model mustn't assign applicant evidence.

Identity matching is deterministic02
The source context

Keep the amount
beside its source

  • Retain the document,
  • field or location,
  • income basis and period for each value.

Let reviewers check the original, not a detached number.

Extraction prepares evidence, not truth certification03
The criteria evidence

Show the rule
and its evidence

Record together:

  • Configured criterion.
  • Value.
  • Classification.

Keep the manager's decision separate; models mustn't invent thresholds or recommend outcomes.

Policy review remains a business responsibility04
The unresolved work

Make missing
evidence explicit

List missing evidence, its follow-up owner and the approved evidence route.

Don't fill blanks with inference to make a summary look finished.

Screening-stage requests require human approval05
The conflicting values

Keep conflicting
values visible

Preserve both sources

and their definitions.

Explain gross-versus-net differences; combining them into one number hides why the file needs review.

Difference ≠ dishonesty06
The human action

Log the human action
separately

Record the authorized reviewer, time, decision and approved conditions in the restricted application record.

Reading output and criteria results aren't human approval.

No AI decision, ranking or recommendation07
A reusable review packet

Build the review packet
around questions

  • Which household?
  • What evidence?
  • Which rule?
  • What's unresolved?
  • Who acts next?
  • Where is the decision recorded?

Keep answers together; don't expose sensitive documents in routine chat.

Template, not a screenshot of a real applicant08
A practical review test

Test whether the file
is reviewable

Use one synthetic or properly redacted example. Trace a value's source, explain a rule result and locate the human action.

Any missing link needs a process fix.
