The pay frequency

Check the
payment frequency

Weekly, every two weeks and twice monthly differ.

Confirm frequency before annualizing regular pay; an unlabeled payment doesn't establish a monthly total.

Period and frequency belong beside the amount05
An illustrative calculation

Frequency changes
the monthly equivalent

Illustrative gross pay: $2,400/period.

Twice monthly:

$2,400 × 24 ÷ 12 =

$4,800/month.

Every two weeks:

$2,400 × 26 ÷ 12 =

$5,200/month.

Annualized, not monthly cash receipts.

The coverage limits

Check what the
source covers

One connected account can omit income deposited elsewhere.

Record covered accounts, periods and unresolved gaps; incomplete coverage isn't the household's total income.

Coverage limits need human review07
The time period

Check timing and
unusual periods

Paystubs and bank history can cover different dates.

Separate bonuses, partial periods and pay changes; one unusual payment doesn't establish recurring income.
