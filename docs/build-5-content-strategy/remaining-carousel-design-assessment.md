## Design choices and comparison

The vendor COI reference uses cream paper, ink typography, strong blue selection
boxes and pen marks, selective gold highlights and a dark emphasis page. This
batch follows that visual grammar without copying its sparse opinion-slide pacing.
The leasing decks retain explanatory lessons, checks and usable record fields.
Blue provides visible emphasis beyond page numbers; gold identifies selected
phrases rather than becoming the dominant decoration. Quiet pages remain valid.

Each deck has one dark page: the final send-time check, human income explanation
and authorized access handover respectively. Marks vary across the whole deck,
not by putting every device on every page. No public page carries an internal
build number. No page-specific font, spacing or color override was introduced.

Teaching type stays at the established 96/120 cover, 78/96 title, 48/72 body and
36/48 metadata scales on 1080 × 1350 pages. Full evidence/signoff labels use a
named shared two-column worksheet modifier at the same body size. Long labels
wrap rather than being abbreviated or shrunk. The eight-field income and
readiness worksheets retain every canonical field and their closing instruction.

The income example keeps both calculations and the annualization caveat together:
$2,400 × 24 ÷ 12 = $4,800/month; $2,400 × 26 ÷ 12 = $5,200/month. It explains
frequency differences, not an applicant threshold or a fraud conclusion.

## Verification evidence

- All three renderer runs passed exact titles, bodies and footers; loaded fonts;
  annotation geometry and wrapping; content-band and footer clearances.
- All three final-PDF verifications passed canonical semantic-region text,
  dimensions, used-font embedding and the single shared grain-image checks.
- Every final-PDF-derived page was inspected at full size: 9 + 11 + 10 = 30 pages.
- Contact sheets and 360px-wide cover, dense example/decision and worksheet
  previews were inspected. No clipped text, wrapped pen target or footer collision
  was observed. Body text remains approximately 16px at that mobile width.
- The vendor COI reference was compared for hierarchy and selective color use.
  Readability and usefulness matter more than matching a decoration count.

During export, a repeated annotation phrase initially decorated both title and
body. Duplicate body decorations were removed from income page three and funds
page five, then both decks were exported and verified again. Canonical wording
did not change. In particular, gross-income remains an intact compound.

## Honest limitations and boundaries

These are useful teaching documents, not evidence of a deployed client system.
The readiness deck explicitly labels proposed production controls and separates
the sandbox signature/welcome demo endpoint from funds, inspections and possession.
AI never makes a rental decision. Individual screening-message approval and the
send-time recheck remain explicit. Scope is communicated in text, not color alone.

The permission worksheet has a complete prose field list rather than a two-column
form. It preserves the compact lesson and review action, but is less scanable than
the income and readiness record grids. Some long worksheet labels wrap across
two lines. Those are deliberate tradeoffs, not reasons to reduce the type size.
Technical checks and this self-assessment cannot establish audience response or
user design acceptance. Nothing has been uploaded or published.
