Use the [manual testing flow](manual-testing-flow.md) for preparation, exact
synthetic inputs and checks. Record only the business-facing flow.

No terminal, script execution, Docker/tunnel startup, n8n editor, database query,
credential screen or API console appears in the video. Tour completion now
happens through the manager chat, so no off-camera simulation command is needed
between the resident steps.

## Before capture

once if necessary. Use worksambhav26@gmail.com as sender and sambhavn8n@gmail.com
as recipient, with fictional calendar organizer Alex Morgan.

Preserve anything you need before resetting demo rows. A reset doesn't erase
Gmail, Telegram history, calendar bookings or old DocuSign envelopes.
Tidy visible mail/chats yourself and free an intended test calendar slot.
Keep unrelated personal conversations and mail off camera.

Open the listing, both Telegram chats, recipient Gmail and Airtable Grid.
Keep technical troubleshooting surfaces closed or outside the captured region.
Use a fresh inquiry and reference, not an old application preview.

describe a shifted time as the exact requested booking.

## Editing and duration

Aim for about 6–8 minutes after trimming form typing, provider waits and tab
switching. Keep each approval and its before/after effect visible.
The unedited rehearsal will be longer because extraction, email and external
services take time. Don't claim a fixed completion time for every run.

The short post is a 30–60 second cut from this same recording, not another
recording: new lead notice → public answer → held tour → manager approval →
evidence card → signature → approved welcome.

## Final check

No private personal mail/chats or credentials on screen. Mock and Sandbox
boundaries are visible and spoken. The manager's control is clear from the
start, every shown business action has its approval, and no script runs between
the inquiry and final welcome.
